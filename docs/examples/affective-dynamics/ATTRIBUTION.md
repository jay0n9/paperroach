## Processed and derivative notes

The Markdown notes are adaptations of the paper created with PaperRoach and a local Ollama model, followed by a public source-review pass. They are distributed under CC BY 4.0. Appropriate credit, a link to the source, the license, and a description of changes are provided here and in each reviewed note.

Changes include removing local-only metadata, correcting formula transcription and classification errors, narrowing causal language, and adding an explicit limitations section. See `CHANGELOG.md` for details.

The machine-generated drafts are included only to make the transformation inspectable. Their warnings and error states are part of the public record.
